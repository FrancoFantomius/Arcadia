Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Welcome to Arcadia. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Arcadia is designed as a local-first application. We do not automatically collect, store, or process any of your document content on our servers. All documents created within Arcadia are saved locally within your browser's local storage.

2. How We Use Your Information

Because we do not collect your personal document data, we do not use it for any purpose. Your data remains strictly on your device unless you manually choose to export or share it.

3. Third-Party Services

Our application may use third-party libraries (such as html2pdf.js) to provide certain functionalities like PDF export. These libraries operate locally on your device and are not configured by us to transmit your data.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
